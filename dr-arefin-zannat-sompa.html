How Consultation Works

A structured, empathetic, and confidential 3-step approach to understanding your symptoms and guiding your mental recovery.

01

Detailed Initial Evaluation

Comprehensive 30 to 40 minute session discussing your symptoms, personal history, stress factors, and emotional challenges in a warm atmosphere.

02

Personalized Medical Plan

Clear explanation of your medical diagnosis using plain, everyday terms along with a tailored medication or therapeutic care roadmap.

03

Follow-up & Recovery Support

Regular review consultations to evaluate progress, fine-tune dosage, and offer continuous family guidance for long-term emotional well-being.

What she treats

Consultations cover diagnosis, medication management, and referral for talk therapy where it helps.

Depression & mood

Low mood, loss of interest, bipolar disorder, and postpartum depression.

Anxiety & panic

Generalised anxiety, panic attacks, phobia, and obsessive-compulsive disorder.

Sleep problems

Insomnia, disturbed sleep cycles, and sleep difficulty linked to stress.

Adolescent mental health

Exam stress, behavioural change, and school or family difficulty in teenagers.

Psychotic illness

Schizophrenia and related conditions, with long-term follow-up and family guidance.

Addiction support

Substance use assessment, withdrawal management, and relapse prevention planning.
